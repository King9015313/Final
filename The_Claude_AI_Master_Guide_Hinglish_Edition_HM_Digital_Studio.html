Same kaam, alag prompt

Guide ka bada hissa yahi sikhata hai: prompt ko clear kaise likhein. Ek chhota example (illustrative):

Kamzor prompt
Write a budget plan for me.

Role, audience, format ya limit kuch nahi bataya, to Claude ko andaza lagana padta hai.

Clear prompt
Role: You are a personal finance coach.Task: Create a simple monthly budget plan.Context: I am a fresher in India. My monthly take-home is [AMOUNT] and my rent is [RENT].Format: A table with Needs, Wants and Savings, then 3 short tips.Limit: Under 200 words, plain language.

Role, task, context, format aur limit di gayi hai. Aise 70 ready prompts guide mein hain.
